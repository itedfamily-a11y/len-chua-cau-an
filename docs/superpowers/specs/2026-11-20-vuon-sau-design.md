# Vườn sau chùa (garden behind the main hall): design

Date: 2026-11-20.

Status:
- On 2026-11-20 the owner asked for a garden courtyard behind the Đại Hùng Bảo Điện with five times the hall's area.
- The owner approved the summary below and the top-down sketch in chat ("Đồng ý", with the Quan Âm statue kept).
- This document is Claude's detailed design. It rests on a read-only map of the code: three files in the session scratchpad, `v7/garden/explore/A-geometry.md`, `B-art-perf.md` and `C-systems-tests.md`.

## Tóm tắt cho chủ trò chơi

**Kích thước và lối vào**
- Phía sau chính điện có thêm một vườn rộng khoảng 5 000 m² (72 × 69 m), gấp 5 lần phần thân chính điện (37 × 26,9 m).
- Vào vườn bằng hai cổng nách ở cuối hai dãy hành lang. Chính điện không mở cửa sau, vì ban thờ đặt sát tường sau.

**Trong vườn**
- Một hồ sen ở giữa. Trên đảo đá giữa hồ có tượng Quan Âm đứng màu trắng, quay về phía chính điện.
- Một thủy tạ lục giác bên hồ, là nơi "Ngồi ngắm hồ sen".
- Cây cối: hàng cây cảnh trên bệ đá, cây bồ đề lớn có ghế đá quanh gốc, rừng trúc ở hai góc, hoa đại, tùng, mai, quất.
- Lối đá và đèn đá, ban đêm sáng ấm, đom đóm bên hồ.

**Người trong vườn**
- Khách hành hương thỉnh thoảng vào dạo và ngồi ghế đá.
- Có thêm dấu ấn "Vườn sau chùa" và mục "Vườn sau chùa" trên bản đồ chùa.
- Người chơi khác (đi chùa chung) cũng vào vườn được.

**Cảnh xung quanh và hiệu năng**
- Sông, hàng cây và núi xa phía sau được dời ra xa cho vừa vườn.
- Vườn thêm không quá 50 nghìn tam giác và không thêm đèn thật. Khi đứng trong vườn, máy không vẽ nội thất chính điện.

## 1. Scope

In scope:
- **The garden enclosure:** a walled garden directly behind the compound's back wall.
- **Access:** two side gates in the back wall, its contents, colliders, ground heights, nav, footsteps, night glows.
- **Activities and crowd:** one new sit activity, one stamp site, one map place, and a garden kind for the AI crowd.
- **Moves outside the walls:** the moves of the river, tree rows, tufts, terrain and far mountains that make room for the garden. This is the same kind of move as the v3 and v4 back-wall moves (`src/AREA2.md`).
- **Docs and tests:** `src/AREA2.md`, `src/API.md`, and headless checks.

Out of scope:
- **Hall:** no door in the hall, and no change to the hall, tiers or altar.
- **Content:** no new music or ambient sound loops, no new UI panels, no new external assets.
- **Multiplayer:** no change to the multiplayer or voice protocol.

## 2. Measures and placement (world coordinates; gate z +31, deeper = more negative)

- **Hall area.** The hall building's outer walls are x ±18.5 and z −86.3…−113.2: 37.0 × 26.9 = 995.3 m². Five times that is 4 976.5 m².
- **Garden interior.** x −36…36 and z −118.3…−187.6, so 72.0 × 69.3 = 4 989.6 m² (5.01 × the hall).
  - The garden starts at the outer face of the existing compound back wall (body z −118.3…−117.7). That wall stays where it is: it hides the sealed marble deck and the open back edge of tier 1 behind the hall.
- **Garden walls.** Same style and height as the compound wall (`wall()` in `05-world.js`).
  - Back wall body z −188.2…−187.6, x −36.6…36.6.
  - Side walls: body x ±(36.0…36.6), z −188.2…−117.7.
  - Two short front walls on the line of the compound back wall, body z −118.3…−117.7, |x| 24.3…36.6. They close the garden where it is wider than the compound.
- **Gates (cổng nách).** Two openings in the compound back wall at the ends of the galleries, x ±(21.4…23.4) (2.0 m clear). Each sits in the free pocket between the last gallery column (x ±20.8) and the side wall (x ±23.7).
  - The wall is split into three segments: x −24.3…−23.4, −21.4…21.4, 23.4…24.3.
  - Each gate has a small tiled roof on two short posts, 2.9 m clear height, in the style of the compound's roofs.
- **Bounds.** `G.collide.bounds` becomes x ±37 and z0 −189. It is set in `00-core.js` and guarded in `08-world2.js`, as v4 did with −119. Remote players are clamped to these bounds by module 22, so they follow automatically.

## 4. Moves outside the walls (like v3 / v4)

Each item names the files to change; exact literals are in `A-geometry.md` section 2.

In the code the hills rise as smoothstep(25, 120, distance to 05's terrain box |x| ≤ 32, z −197.2…77), so the flat area is larger: |x| ≤ 57 and z ≥ −222.2 along the axes, rounded at the corners (+0.19 m at (57, −222.2)).

- **River (13, and its copy in 05's KEEP list).**
  - The middle reach moves so its water edge stays at least 20 m from the garden walls: behind the back wall (z ≤ −208.2) and beside the side walls (|x| ≥ 56.6 where z > −208).
  - The reaches far from the garden keep their control points.
  - Banks of non-adjacent reaches stay at least 14 m apart.
  - The two boats and the firefly area follow the river.
  - The reeds and the `EXC` circles keep their rules.
- **Scenery (13).**
  - The clear walking strip moves outside the garden walls.
  - `canPlace` treats the garden rect as inside.
  - The tree rows behind the back wall move behind the garden (z ≈ −194 / −199 / −204, skipped near the river).
  - The single back tree strip and the back tufts move behind the garden.
  - Side tree strips and tufts run along the garden sides.
  - The far tree patches avoid the garden rect plus a margin.
  - Stork flock 3 must not fly through the Bồ đề canopy or the statue.
- **Far mountains (05).** The KEEP points gain the garden corners and the moved river line. Cones that touch them are pushed outward and scaled as in v4 (k ≤ 1.6 instead of 1.4, foot narrowed up to 40 %; deviation 2, section 10); all other cones stay identical.
- **Shadows (05).** The sun shadow box follows the player into the garden (clamp z −94 → −160). The postcard target is unchanged.
- **Post-processing (07-hau-ky).** The "inside the hall" camera zone becomes the hall interior only: |x| < 18.5 and −113.2 < z < −84. A camera behind the player in the garden never turns on the hall's AO and bloom.
- **Hall interior culling (18).** The interior and the altar (about 164 k triangles) are not drawn while the camera is behind the hall's back wall (z < −113.2). The hall's back wall is solid and its windows are dark slabs, so nothing visible is lost.
- **Footsteps (10).** The 'stone' rect gains the garden paths; the garden lawn stays 'grass'.

## 5. Activities, crowd, map, stamps

- **Map ("Bản đồ chùa").** A new place "Vườn sau chùa" at the bonsai terrace (0, −124.5). Auto-walk from anywhere reaches it through the galleries and a gate.
- **Stamp ("Dấu ấn hành hương").** A new site "Vườn sau chùa" for the garden rect, with a short intro in `SITE_INFO`: "Hồ sen, cây bồ đề và tượng Quan Âm giữa hồ. Nơi dừng chân thong thả, nghe gió qua rừng trúc." (the toast already names the site above the intro; the first draft repeated the name and was 118 characters)
- **Activity "Ngồi ngắm hồ sen".**
  - Where: the POI at the thủy tạ (stand (16.2, −150), facing the statue).
  - What it does: the player sits on the bench, the camera frames the pond and the statue, and a few calm lines guide slow breathing. It follows the pattern of the existing bench-breathing activity in `50-activities-c.js` ("startBreath").
  - Text tone: respectful and calm, no promise of any result. It ends on movement or "Đứng dậy".
  - POI rings: the cap of 16 rings in `00-core.js` grows so the new POI gets its ring.
- **Crowd (20).**
  - A new destination kind `vuon` holds the garden benches and about 8 stroll points along the paths.
  - It has its own weight (3 / 1 / 3 for adults / children / elders; deviation 4, section 10), so a fraction of the pilgrims visits the garden: about 30.8 % of the visitors pass through it during a stay, 4.4 % of the spot choices (the first reading, "roughly one pilgrim in ten", was 12 % of the visitors). Its own distance discount keeps the long walk from ruling it out.
  - Pilgrims reach the garden by the galleries and the gates, like the player.
- **Multiplayer.** Nothing to add beyond the bounds. Remote players' feet use `G.heightAt`, which includes the thủy tạ floor.

## 6. Module and boot

- **New file `src/19-vuon-sau.js` with `buildVuonSau(G)`.**
  - It builds the garden, the gates and the garden walls.
  - It registers colliders, nav nodes and edges, spots, the POI handle, footstep paths, glows and the firefly area.
  - It installs a `G.heightAt` wrapper for the pavilion floor and the steps.
  - It exposes `G.vuonSau` with the numbers other modules and tests read (rect, gates, pond, island, pavilion, spots, `surfaceAt(x, z)`).
- **Boot step "vườn sau chùa"** in `99-boot.js`: after "điện phụ & tháp Văn Thù" (17) and before "trang trí sân chùa" (12). 12 and 13 sample nav, spots and colliders at build time, so they must see the garden.
- **Contract.** `src/API.md` gets the contract, in the style of the other modules. `src/AREA2.md` gets an area row and a "What moved with the garden (v5)" list.
- **Code style.** House style: `safe()` around the parts, a triangle tally like `19-cung-dinh.js`, and no Vietnamese UI text outside the activity, stamp and map strings.

## 7. Error handling and edge cases

- **One part fails.** If any garden part throws, `safe()` logs it to `G.errors` and the rest of the game builds; the gates still open onto plain lawn.
- **Night.** The lanterns use glow sprites like the compound's lamps, and the fireflies use the existing pool and night rule.
- **Camera.** The garden walls get camera blockers only if the follow camera can pass through them in the checks (the compound walls have none today). It can: with the player against the back wall the camera stood 6.5 m outside it, 4.5 m high, where the new tree rows stand (g01 B23, measured on the build before the blockers), so the five garden walls have blockers (deviation 5, section 10).
- **Old saves.** No save field is added; the stamp list grows by one site and old saves read it as not yet stamped.
- **Old builds in a shared room.** They clamp a player who is in the garden to z −119. This is accepted: old builds vanish once the page reloads.

## 9. Risks

- **Phones.** The scene is already about 466 k triangles; phone frame rate has never been measured. The interior cull from the garden saves more than the garden adds. The hall-court view gains only the always-drawn far group (walls, gates, paths, rim and water: about 7 k triangles and 5 calls); the garden's decor, trees included, is drawn only from camera y ≥ 16 or when the camera is not in front of / inside the hall (the garden's own per-frame rule, item 7 of section 10).
- **The long walk.** The garden is reached only by the galleries, a walk of about 100 m from the hall court. The map's auto-walk covers it.
- **The statue.** A low-poly human figure is the hardest art piece. If the visual check finds it undignified, it is simplified (robe silhouette, no detailed face) rather than dropped. Dropping it would need the owner.

## 10. Deviations recorded during implementation (the owner's rule: Claude decides, the decision is written down)

1. **Pond water at y 0.04, not −0.25.** The visible ground mesh (y −0.03, 10 m cells) has no hole, so a water plane at −0.25 would be invisible; the water lies above the lawn plate (y 0.006) like the Long Trì pond. The rim top stays y 0.25. `G.vuonSau.pond.water` is 0.04 and g01 B02 asserts it.
2. **Far-mountain cap k ≤ 1.6, not 1.4.** With the river 70 m further back, cone 24 stands right behind it and cannot clear it at 1.4 even with the foot narrowed to 60 %. Five of the 34 cones change (23 to 27), the other 29 are identical. The five end 306 to 360 m from (0, 15), about the day fog distance (330 m): by day they read as a hazy backdrop.
3. **The scenery of the whole world is re-rolled.** From the tufts onward 13-scenery's shared random stream is consumed differently (the regions of tufts, tree rows and strips changed, and the wider bounds widen the rice-field "near" band), so those items stand in new places everywhere, the front yard the owner had seen included. Villages, houses, the entrance planting and the "đất nện" patches keep their numbers. The bounds x ±37 also make the walkable front yard 6 m wider on each side.
4. **Crowd weights 3 / 1 / 3 (first 2 / 0.6 / 2).** "Roughly one pilgrim in ten" has two readings: 12 % of the visitors (weights 1 / 0.3 / 1, 1.5 % of the choices) or the share of choices. The weights 2 / 0.6 / 2 were chosen first (model: about 22 % of the visitors, 2.9 % of the choices), but a 480 s run gave only about 1.1 % of the choices (3 to 7 garden choices of 300 to 360; the model's positions of the compound's spots are approximate), so g01 D03 (at least 3 garden choices and at least 1 %) failed in about 2 of 5 runs on an unchanged build. The weights were raised to 3 / 1 / 3 (model: about 30.8 % of the visitors, 4.4 % of the choices). Measured on the final build: 4 to 9 of 301 to 344 choices (2.0 % pooled over seven g01 runs, 2.8 % in the measuring run), D03 passed in all seven g01 runs (the lowest count was 4, which is 1.2 % and close to the floor); the exact figures are in AREA2.md. Even with 3 / 1 / 3 the old D03 floor was effectively 4 choices (1 % of 330 is 3.3) and the counts of eleven runs were 4, 9, 8, 7, 8, 6, 4, 4, 10, 8, 9 (mean 7), so about 5–8 % of the whole-g01 runs would have failed on an unchanged build. The final review therefore lowered D03 to at least 2 garden choices and at least 0.5 % (ceiling 30 % unchanged) and updated its label to the 3 / 1 / 3 model.
5. **Camera blockers on the five garden walls**, because the follow camera does pass through them (section 7).
6. **The activity "Ngồi ngắm hồ sen" changes no journal counter**: it is sitting, not the 4 / 6 s breathing exercise.
7. **The garden hides its own decor, and the mai cap is raised.** The hall interior culling of 18-chinh-dien.js (nothing of the interior is drawn behind z −113.2) is a different thing. The hiding of the garden decor is the garden's own per-frame rule (`19-vuon-sau.js`, `G.onUpdate` order 76): `groups.near` (decor, lanterns' stone bodies, gold) is hidden while the camera is below y 16 in front of the hall (z > −75, |x| < 20.4) or inside it (z > −113.2, |x| < 18.4); walls, gates, paths, rim and water (`groups.far`) always stay. The cap of mai petal sources in 12-decor-inside.js is 40 + the garden's own count (13) = 53; the pool of falling petals (07-fx `PN`) is 100 instead of 80 so the court keeps its density.
8. **The statue's shawl is part of the robe lathe.** The plan drew the shawl as a second, open lathe (robe 396 + shawl 144 triangles, z scale 0.85). Its profile ran from the neck down, so all 144 faces were wound inward: culled from the camera but still written to the sun shadow map, about 0.1 m in front of the robe, which speckled the chest. Now robe and shawl are ONE closed 18-sided lathe (504 triangles, z scale 0.88, slopes ≤ 0.6, the shawl part a lighter tone): the statue is 1 688 triangles (was 1 724), 3.0 m to the crown and 3.25 m to the top of the halo. The garden measures 29 402 triangles in node with three r180 and 30 772 in the game (r160), 8 meshes; the 4.7 % difference was not traced to a cause. `G.vuonSau.tris` (30 772) is the reference.
9. **The path mesh has its own material** (`MeshLambertMaterial`, vertex colours, polygonOffset −2 / −2) so the flagstones do not flicker against the lawn plate 1.4 cm below them; every other garden mesh uses the shared `G.mats.*`.
10. **Collision circles of the pavilion are r 0.35, not the drawn thickness (piers 0.22, rails 0.12), and two collider-only boxes close the front stubs.** The player (r 0.35) moves one step per frame and G.collide.resolve pushes out in two sequential passes; against thin overlapping circles that leaks at a step of about 0.06 m (45 fps) or more, so at 30 fps phones or at "Nhanh" 4 m/s the avatar slid through the rail and could stand in the lotus pond. With r 0.35 the chain is closed up to 0.2 m per frame (`tests-local/voice/g03-rail-sweep.mjs`); the avatar stops about 0.7 m from the drawn rail. The walking bounds (x ±37) keep the player's centre at ±36.65 while the garden side walls end at ±36.6, which left a 0.05 m gap at the front-stub corners; two boxes x ±(36.6…37.4), z −118.3…−117.7 close it (the garden now registers 92 colliders, 90 before).
11. **Smaller points recorded in the final review.** The avatar hovers up to 0.15 m over the first two pavilion steps (the linear `G.heightAt` ramp, as with the other stone stairs, house style). The wider bounds also make 13 more of the 64 "old" outside trees (x 30.65…36.65) walkable; like 13 others (x 24.65…30.65) in v4 they have no collider. The pavilion lantern hangs from the roof shell (rod 2.02 m, lowest point 1.84 m above the floor). Texts: the stamp-book line reads "Đã nhận N/8 dấu ấn hành hương", `LINES.vuon` names no time of day, tutorial step 2 mentions the garden. Footstep rule of 10-audio.js starts at z > −118.3. The share-photo shadow target follows the snapshot camera. The published river samples carry only {x, z, nx, nz, hw, lv}. Not done on purpose: the seated activity camera has no portrait variant, and two friends who both sit in the pavilion share one bench spot (as at the stupa-garden bench).

## 11. Dải lụa cầu an và hoa đăng trong vườn (2026-11-23)

The owner's request (Vietnamese): "Có chuyển hồ thả hoa đăng ra sau hồ quan âm đằng sau đại hùng bảo điện và viết thư chúc phúc trên cây ở mặt trước sang cây đại thụ đằng sau đại hùng bảo điện, bỏ bớt dây treo nhìn rối". Decisions K1–K13 were made by the controller (the owner's rule: decide, write it down); every number the planner changed after a numeric check is listed at the end.

- **Meaning.** "Thả hoa đăng" (POI `hoa-dang`: stand, release point, 18 visitor lanterns, the player's lanterns) moves to the Quan Âm pond. "Viết lời cầu an" (POI `cau-an`: red silk ribbons) moves to the garden bodhi at the planter. "Bỏ bớt dây treo" = far fewer ribbons, spaced so that they read as separate strips. The bodhi and the lotus pond in the front court stay as scenery (same models, colliders, crowd spots, benches), with no ribbon, lantern or POI left. (Superseded on 2026-11-25: the owner asked to delete both; see section 12.)
- **Ownership.** `05-world.js` also publishes its objects as `W.frontTree` / `W.frontPond`. `19-vuon-sau.js` builds `G.vuonSau.ribbonTree` and `G.vuonSau.lanternPond` (same shapes) and assigns them to `W.tree` / `W.pond` only when the parts they need ('vật cản', 'dẫn đường', 'bồn bồ đề' + 'cây bồ đề'; 'vật cản', 'dẫn đường', 'bờ hồ' + 'đảo đá') built without error, so a garden failure leaves the activities in the front court (since 2026-11-25 there is no front object: a garden failure means no ribbon or lantern activity). `40-activities-b.js` still reads `W.tree` / `W.pond` once at install. Scenery placement in 12 / 13 reads every `W[k].poi` / `.stand`, so the front objects keep the front clearings and the placement of the whole world does not move.
- **Lantern bank.** Stand (0, −136.9) yaw π, poi (0, −136.4) r 3.0, release (0, −139.1) (1.1 m past the water edge), on the north bank at the end of the axis path, facing the statue across the water; camera pos [2.6, 3.4, −131.6] look [−0.6, 0.6, −145] (player, release point and statue head in frame at 16:9, 4:3 and both phone sizes; no lamp or bench in the way). Lotus pads, flowers and buds keep > 1.95 m from the release point (buds and stalks are offset by 0.15 m, so they stay ≥ 1.8 m); the layout is re-rolled by that rejection, still 60 pads, nearest pad 2.28 m. The crowd stroll spot (0, −134.5) moves to (−3.4, −135.2), facing the statue.

When the player comes within 30 m of the statue (and the browser's data-saver flag is off) it imports `three/addons/loaders/GLTFLoader.js` and `three/addons/libs/meshopt_decoder.module.js` and loads one of two copies of the photogrammetry scan "Tuong Phat Ba 5K" by vudn54 (Sketchfab uid da1a8fe064df40bf892adb03fa115e3d, CC BY 4.0): `assets/mo-hinh/quan-am-but-thap-4k.glb` (150 000 triangles, 4096 px WebP texture, 3.6 MB) on desktops, or `assets/mo-hinh/quan-am-but-thap.glb` (100 000 triangles, 2048 px, 1.9 MB) on touch devices, at low quality, or when the GPU's texture limit is under 4096 (`pickUrl()`). Both use EXT_meshopt_compression, KHR_mesh_quantization and EXT_texture_webp, and are normalised offline: y up, facing +z, base at y = 0, centred, height 1. The source (the owner downloaded Sketchfab's "GLB, texture size 8k", 63 MB) has 499 998 triangles in 22 pieces, per-face normals (1.4 M vertices for 247 000 positions, hence `--smooth-normals`), one 8192 px JPEG, real size (3.885 m with the floor patch), and faces 59.3° off +z. Conversion: `--rot 0,59.3,0 --crop-ymin 0.057` (removes the brick floor patch and the row of footing stones; the red-lacquer base then stands on the hall's pedestal and the statue is 3.66 m tall, so the fit to 3.26 × 1.12 is almost 1:1), the wooden post and block that hold the aureole are kept, `--keep-largest 1 --smooth-normals --meshopt --q 85`. Below about 80 000 triangles the UV islands make the simplifier fail (a 60 000 target stopped at 76 000 with 3.5 % error). The module fits the model to the old statue's height (3.26 local units, about 3.65 m after the hall's ×1.12), turns an unlit material into a `MeshStandardMaterial`, retunes it (roughness 0.9, no metalness and no environment map, base colour × 0.45 for the lit part, emissive map = base map at 0.55, so the scan's own baked light and colours carry the look; chosen from five sets rendered in game), adds the model under `W.ext.quanAm.statue`, hides the code-built children and frees their GPU buffers.
